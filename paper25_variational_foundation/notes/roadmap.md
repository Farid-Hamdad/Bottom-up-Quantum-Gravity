# Paper 25 — Roadmap

## Purpose

Paper 25 is the foundation paper of the Bottom-Up Quantum Gravity program.

Its purpose is to reformulate BuP as a variational theory of entanglement equilibrium whose smooth local limit is an effective Einstein equation.

The central equation is:

\[
\frac{\delta S_{\rm BuP}[W,\rho]}{\delta W_{ij}}=0.
\]

The continuum target is:

\[
G_{\mu\nu}[g^{\rm ent}]
+
\Lambda_{\rm ent}g_{\mu\nu}^{\rm ent}
=
8\pi G_{\rm eff}T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm ent}
+
\mathcal H_{\mu\nu}^{\rm BuP}.
\]
