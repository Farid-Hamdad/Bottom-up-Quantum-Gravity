# Paper 25 — Referee Notes and Risk Register

## Main referee risk

Paper 25 is ambitious. The main risk is that a reader interprets it as claiming a complete rigorous derivation of Einstein gravity.

This must be avoided.

The correct claim is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{BuP has a coherent variational chain toward an effective Einstein equation, but the full theorem remains open.}
}
\]

---

## Risk 1 — “This is not a proof of Einstein gravity”

### Expected objection

The paper assembles numerical evidence, calibrated proxies and phenomenological branches, but does not prove the Einstein equation rigorously.

### Response

Correct. Paper 25 explicitly states a theorem target, not a completed theorem.

The purpose is to organize the precise mathematical assumptions needed for the theorem.

---

## Risk 2 — “The correction tensor sectors are heterogeneous”

### Expected objection

\[
H_{\rm spec}, H_{\rm source}, H_{\rm dim}, H_{\rm nonlocal}
\]

are not all measured as tensor norms.

### Response

Correct. Paper 20 distinguishes controlled residuals from phenomenological proxies.

Paper 25 keeps this distinction and identifies the homogeneous tensor norm as an open problem.

---

## Risk 3 — “\(T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm ent}\) is not yet constructed”

### Expected objection

Paper 18 gives a modular scalar source, not a full stress-energy tensor.

### Response

Correct. Paper 25 calls it a modular-source precursor.

The construction of the full tensor \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm ent}\) is listed as a major open problem.

---

## Risk 4 — “\(c_N\) is fitted, not derived”

### Expected objection

The convergence \(c_NL_N\to-\Delta_g\) requires a normalization not yet derived analytically.

### Response

Correct. This is the highest-priority open problem and is the natural target of Paper 26.

---

## Risk 5 — “Ollivier--Ricci only gives a mean calibrated proxy”

### Expected objection

Paper 17 does not prove pointwise convergence to \(R_{\mu\nu}\).

### Response

Correct. Paper 25 states that Paper 17 gives a mean-level affine calibration.

Pointwise/tensorial Ricci reconstruction is listed as an open mathematical debt.

---

## Risk 6 — “SPARC, SLACS and cosmology are phenomenological”

### Expected objection

These branches do not prove the microscopic theory.

### Response

Correct. Paper 25 separates:

- controlled numerical convergence,
- finite-size weak-field precursors,
- calibrated proxies,
- phenomenological validations.

The observational sectors test consequences of the framework, not the rigorous derivation itself.

---

## Risk 7 — “Too many sectors”

### Expected objection

The project contains many papers and sectors.

### Response

Paper 25 reduces them to one organizing chain:

\[
W_{ij}=I(i:j)
\to
L_{\rm ent}, \kappa^{OR}, K_A, L_{\rm ent}^{+}
\to
g_{\mu\nu}, R_{\mu\nu}, T_{\mu\nu}, \Phi_{\rm BuP}
\to
G_{\mu\nu}
+
\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=
8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}
+
\mathcal H_{\mu\nu}.
\]

---

## Recommended wording

Use:

“theorem target”

“controlled numerical evidence”

“modular-source precursor”

“phenomenological proxy”

“effective Einstein limit”

Avoid:

“complete proof”

“fully derived Einstein gravity”

“exact tensor norm”

“final theory”

---

## Final referee-safe claim

\[
\boxed{
\text{Paper 25 establishes a unified variational architecture for BuP and identifies the exact mathematical debts required to turn it into a rigorous continuum theorem.}
}
\]
